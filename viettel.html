Turning operational friction into a more structured system. A focus on contract administration, document validation, and data processing.

02

Context &
Problem

At Viettel Commerce, managing commercial contracts involves a complex web of administration, document validation, and financial reconciliation. The environment is highly operational, requiring precision across numerous concurrent tasks.

A significant point of friction was identified in the warranty processing workflow. The manual handling of device records, including serial-number validation and warranty data extraction, was highly time-consuming and prone to human error when dealing with large volumes of hardware.

Processing hundreds of records manually created a bottleneck.
03

Investigation

To address the bottleneck, the first step was to deconstruct the existing workflow. I observed the manual steps taken to extract warranty data and validate serial numbers.

The root of the inefficiency was not a lack of effort, but a lack of structural automation. Data was treated as raw, unstructured information that required manual processing at every step of the validation chain.

I needed to understand the data requirements of the manufacturer and how our internal records mapped to those requirements, specifically analyzing FX variance calculations and document validation protocols.

04

System Thinking

Instead of simply trying to process the manual workload faster, I looked at the workflow as a pipeline. The goal was to transform raw, noisy inputs into validated, structured outputs with minimal human intervention.

05

Solution

I developed a manufacturer API prototype designed specifically for warranty data extraction and processing.

The prototype automated the pipeline. It ingested the raw serial numbers, interfaced with the manufacturer's data structures, validated the inputs against expected formats, and output a clean, accurate list of warranty statuses.

This shifted the role of the administrator from manual data entry to exception handling and final review, significantly elevating the efficiency of the commercial contract administration process.

06

Impact

The prototype successfully handled the workload. The most significant outcome was the drastic reduction in processing time and the elimination of manual transcription errors, leading to improved serial-number-list accuracy.
